## 2. The Problem (What Other Streaming Platforms Do)
Most streaming platforms treat your living room like a noisy casino floor:
- **Uninvited noise**: Hovering over a poster unleashes loud music and abrupt video clips while you are just trying to read the synopsis.
- **Rushed endings**: An emotional film reaches its poignant final scene, and an anxious 5-second countdown instantly shrinks the screen and kills the mood.
- **Geofenced lockouts**: Traveling for work, visiting family, or studying at university results in frustrating "household" lockouts and 7-day verification codes.

---

## 3. How It Works (The 3-Step Core Loop)
1. **Browse in peace**: Discover curated films by genre, director, or mood. No audio plays until you click play.
2. **Watch with clarity**: Enjoy crisp 4K streaming with dialogue speech boost and customizable caption contrast.
3. **Let the story breathe**: End credits roll naturally with the composer's complete score. Advance to the next episode only when you are ready.

---

## 4. Key Differentiators (Built from Real User Feedback)
- **Instant Autoplay Mute Switch**: One permanent toggle in the navigation bar silences all previews across your entire account.
- **Respectful End Credits Mode**: No countdown alarms. Sit with the ending and listen to the soundtrack until the very last credit.
- **One-Click Hide**: Don't like a title or genre? Click "Hide" on any card to permanently remove it from your recommendations.
- **Dialogue Clarity Booster**: Built-in audio compressor normalizes volume, elevating quiet whisper dialogues while softening explosive sound effects.
- **True Anywhere Streaming**: Stream simultaneously on any device, anywhere in the world. Zero geographic IP tethering or extra-member penalties.
